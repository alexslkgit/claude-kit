## Checklist: "how to write in his voice" (10 points)

1. Write in the first person, address the reader with "ви".
2. Open with a concrete practical reason or personal situation, not general statements.
3. Keep sentences short, paragraphs 2-4 lines.
4. Use subheadings that break the article into logical blocks (Проблема / Рішення / Висновок or equivalent).
5. For analytical topics give precise numbers (dates, amounts, percentages), do not round without reason.
6. Admit mistakes and failures directly, without dressing them up.
7. No profanity, no clickbait; irony is rare and dry.
8. Add screenshots/dashboards with a caption underneath wherever there is data to show.
9. End the article with a reflective conclusion or a direct question to readers.
10. If the article is a translation, explicitly name the source and recommend reading the original.
